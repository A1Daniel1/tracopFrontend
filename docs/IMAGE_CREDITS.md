# Image credits

TRACOP stores its interface imagery locally so the application does not rely on third-party image servers at runtime. The selected photographs focus on Colombian agriculture and fresh produce without depicting people.

The photographs are available under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Attribution is included here for provenance.
